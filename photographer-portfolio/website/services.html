From Enquiry to Archive

The Process

  1. Initial Conversation

    We speak via phone or coffee to see if our aesthetic and temperament align. I share unedited full-gallery proofs so you can inspect realistic deliverables from start to finish.

  2. Date Confirmation

    To hold your date exclusively, we execute a simple digital agreement and a 30% retainer. Your date is permanently blocked on my calendar and no other bookings are accepted.

  3. The Gathering

    Before the day, we review the family schedule and natural lighting times. On the day, I work seamlessly alongside your family without demanding staged re-enactments.

  4. Handcrafted Delivery

    A sneak preview arrives within 72 hours. Your complete private web gallery goes live in 4 to 6 weeks, ready for high-resolution single or full zip archive download.

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

How many photographs will we receive?

For a typical single-day wedding, you will receive between 500 and 700 finished photographs. For two days, 800 to 1,200. I believe in quality over reckless quantity: every image delivered is culled, individually adjusted for color, contrast, and skin tone, and ready for fine-art printing.

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Yes. Roughly half of my commissions are outside Mumbai—frequently in Udaipur, Goa, Jaipur, Kerala, and internationally. Travel and accommodation expenses are billed simply at cost with zero markup.

Do you provide RAW files?

I deliver finished master-resolution JPEGs. The editing and tonal curve are half of the artistic voice of the studio. Just as a chef does not serve raw flour and butter, I do not provide raw camera sensor files.

How does the client download portal work?

You and your family receive a secure link to your private gallery. You can view all images on desktop or mobile, mark favorites with a heart icon for album design, and download either individual high-res files or the full collection as a ZIP archive directly to your hard drive.

What is required to book a date?

A date is confirmed upon signature of our plain-English commission contract and receipt of the 30% retainer. The remaining balance is due two weeks before your first event date.
