The Studio Practice
[120 MEDIUM FORMAT / 35MM PRIMES]

I make photographs of marriages, solitary portraits, and places with long memory. The approach is deliberate, unobtrusive, and unhurried.

A wedding is not a production set. My work centers on the quiet gestures between formal events—the mother holding a pallu, two cousins laughing behind the marquee, the fifteen unscripted minutes after the rituals when the dust settles.

Working with available light and medium format digital equipment, I deliver complete documentary records alongside curated albums bound in linen. Based in Mumbai; available wherever your gathering happens.

Ethos & Technique

How I Work

01 / Preparation

Before the day

We speak about the rhythm of the day, family arrangements, and what matters to you. I scout locations beforehand and plan around natural light transitions so nothing feels rushed.

02 / Observation

On the day

I work with quiet prime lenses and minimal gear. No bulky rigs, no constant directions, and no interrupting conversations. The best photographs happen when people forget the lens exists.

03 / Archival Delivery

Afterwards

Every frame is individually color graded with an archival palette. You receive an online gallery with direct full-resolution downloads and a private selection tool for print albums.
