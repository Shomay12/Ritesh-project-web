About This Project

Ritesh Farswan Photography · Digital Platform

When architecting the digital monograph and studio operating suite for Ritesh Farswan Photography, the objective was uncompromising: reject contemporary AI template uniformity and build an authentic tribute to the tactile heritage of medium format 35mm film photography.

Every dimension of this platform was engineered to reflect the precision of an analog darkroom:

  • Acoustic Leaf Shutter Feedback Synthesizing mechanical leaf shutter clicks directly in the browser via the Web Audio API without audio file latency.
  • Continuous Ambient Acoustic Suite Traditional wedding background soundscape playing continuously across page transitions with state persistence.
  • Analog Safelight Darkroom Mode Instant amber safelight palette transformation with zero reflow, simulating physical darkroom paper development.
  • Private Client Proofing & Archival Secured tokenized client portal with favorites marking, feedback notes, and ZIP master deliverable downloads backed by Supabase.
  • Studio Backoffice Suite Full administrative control panel for date availability, gallery management, photo upload pipelines, and automated transactional emails.

The result is a unified platform where fine-art documentary photography and high-performance software engineering exist in total harmony.
